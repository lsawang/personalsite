Building a place for musicians to connect and be heard

Harmonize: helping student musicians find honest feedback and real collaborators, not just likes.

The Problem

College musicians have music, but nowhere to put it.

We interviewed 5 student musicians. To get feedback, most send tracks to friends or post on Discord and wait.

Those channels weren’t built for feedback:

File size limitsVague reactions (“it’s good”)No way to judge a reviewer

How might we build a platform where musicians can share unfinished work, get feedback they can actually use, and find people to make music with?

What Testing Changed

Our testers didn’t use the app the way we expected.

We tested with two people. The TikTok-style feed made the app easy to pick up, but it read as just another social app, and the features that make Harmonize different went unnoticed.

Visual Identity

Not like every other music app.

Most networking apps lean on cool blues and grays. People would be sharing half-finished songs with strangers, so we went warm and friendly instead of LinkedIn-professional.
