Overview

Triceratops is a UC Davis club that crochets dinosaurs and hides them around campus to be found and collected.

They ran on Instagram and Discord with drop announcements, rules, flash sales, and trading. Previously, there was no website, so anyone outside the Discord had no way in.

We built a public site that answers the Discord’s most-asked questions before someone has to ask them.

Problem

How might we: make joining a dino hunt obvious to someone who has never heard of the club?

Rules live in multiple, scattered Discord channels. The post-claim process is nowhere, and the Instagram-to-Discord handoff is invisible.

Newcomers also arrived with three common misconceptions: you need to know how to crochet, meetings are mandatory, and it costs money to join.

Design Decisions

Skim first, depth underneath

Taking a step back

My first hi-fi design leaned on animations mostly: hover states on every card, pop-ups, and reveal animations. Put together, they made the page busy and competed with the content. I cut it back to hover states only so it gave the page a more “premium-feel.”

✗ Rejected

Animation on everything

Hover effects, pop-ups, and animated reveals across the page. Fun in isolation, but stacked together they lowered the premium feel and pulled attention away from the content people came for.

✓ Chosen

Animations where it made sense

Kept the whimsy in illustration, color, and copy, and limited motion to hover states on things you can click. There is such a thing as too much, and restraint is what made the site feel finished.
